Equations

What is an Equation?

An equation is a mathematical statement that shows two expressions are equal.

Example

2x + 4 = 10

Solving Equations

To solve an equation, isolate the variable by performing inverse operations.

2x + 4 = 10

2x = 6
x = 3

Key Idea

Whatever you do to one side of the equation, you must do to the other side.
