Inequalities

What is an Inequality?

An inequality compares two expressions using symbols like <, >, ≤, or ≥.

Examples

x + 3 < 7

2y ≥ 10

Solving Inequalities

Solve inequalities similarly to equations, but be careful when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.

x + 3 < 7

x < 4

Important Rule

When multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number, reverse the inequality sign.

-2x > 6

x < -3
