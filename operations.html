Operations (Multiply, Divide, Add, Subtract)

Order of Operations

After brackets and exponents, we solve multiplication and division from left to right, followed by addition and subtraction from left to right.

Example 1

8 + 4 × 2

= 8 + 8
= 16

Example 2

20 ÷ 5 + 3

= 4 + 3
= 7

Important Rule

Multiplication and division are done in order from left to right, not based on which comes first.
