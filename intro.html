Introduction to PEMDAS

What is PEMDAS?

PEMDAS is the order of operations used to solve mathematical expressions correctly.

PEMDAS stands for:

  • P – Parentheses (Brackets)
  • E – Exponents
  • M – Multiplication
  • D – Division
  • A – Addition
  • S – Subtraction

Example

2 + 3 × 4 = 2 + 12 = 14
